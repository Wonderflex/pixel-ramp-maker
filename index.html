How Every Feature Works

Base Color & Ramp Steps

Pick your anchor color using the color swatch, hex box, screen Eyedropper / Pick, or live H / S / V gradient sliders. The ± input sets how many darker and lighter shades branch out from the base color.

Shading Shifts & Curves

Click Shifts ▾ on a row to steer Hue, Saturation, and Value toward shadow and highlight targets per step. Choose a shift preset or click any mini curve square to edit its Cubic Bézier easing.

Tie to Row 1

Enable Tie to row 1 on any additional row so it automatically shares Row 1's step count and shading shift curves while keeping its own base color—useful for consistent lighting across materials.

Harmonies & Preset Sets

Use Harmonies & Sets ▾ to generate new rows derived from Row 1—including Analogous, Complementary, Split, Triadic, and Tetradic schemes, or utility ramps like Auto rainbow, Graynbow, and Natural tones.

Locking Swatches

Click any generated shade to freeze its current hex value with a 🔒 badge. Locked shades stay fixed while you tweak base colors or shift curves around them; click again to unlock.

Color Budget & Reduce Palette

Set a Budget cap to track total unique colors. Click Auto-reduce to merge the closest pairs automatically, or expand Reduce Palette to inspect perceptual ΔE pairs and combine matches manually without altering grid layout.

Dither Pairs & Vision Views

Expand Dither Pairs to preview 50/50 checkerboards of adjacent (or skip-one) shades and judge smooth blending vs. harsh grain. Switch View in the top bar to test Grayscale and colorblindness without affecting exports.

Export & Embedded PNG Projects

Set swatch size to 1 px for a 1-pixel-per-color sprite palette. Blank duplicates replaces repeated colors with transparent cells. Export .gpl or .hex directly for Aseprite, or use Save PNG (+Project) to embed your full editable project inside the PNG image.
